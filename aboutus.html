ABOUT US

Goal

Our goal is to help busy college students cook quick and simple meals without spending hours in the kitchen. Every recipe is designed to save time, effort and money, so students can eat well while balancing their studies.

What drives us?

We are all individuals who have been to college, and we know how crucial time is. Between classes, assignments and everything else, cooking often gets pushed aside. This drives us to create recipes that are easy to follow, use few ingredients and take very little time.
